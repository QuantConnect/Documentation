The deployment Id identifies a live deployment of your project. To get it in your algorithm, use the AlgorithmIdalgorithm_id property. In live trading, it's the Id of the current deployment, for example L-6bf91128391608d0728ff90b81bfca41. In backtests, it's the backtest Id.

Log($"Deployment Id: {AlgorithmId}");
self.log(f'Deployment Id: {self.algorithm_id}')

Use it to label the data your algorithm saves, so you can tell the results of successive deployments apart.

ObjectStore.Save($"{AlgorithmId}/equity", Portfolio.TotalPortfolioValue.ToStringInvariant());
self.object_store.save(f'{self.algorithm_id}/equity', str(self.portfolio.total_portfolio_value))

To get the deployment Id outside of your algorithm, see Get Deployment Id.
