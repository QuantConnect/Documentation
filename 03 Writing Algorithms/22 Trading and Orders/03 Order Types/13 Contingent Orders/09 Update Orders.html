You can update any open contingent order, including a child order that waits for its parent to fill. To update an order, pass an UpdateOrderFields object to the Updateupdate method on the OrderTicket, or call one of its update methods. An update doesn't release a held order. If you give a held limit order a marketable price, the order fills only after its parent fills.

// Move the take profit of a bracket order while the entry waits to fill.
var response = tickets[1].UpdateLimitPrice(price * 1.03m, "Updated take profit");
if (response.IsSuccess)
{
    Debug("Take profit updated successfully");
}
# Move the take profit of a bracket order while the entry waits to fill.
response = tickets[1].update_limit_price(price * 1.03, "Updated take profit")
if response.is_success:
    self.debug("Take profit updated successfully")

Some brokerages don't support updates to contingent orders or to their quantity. To change these orders, cancel the set and place it again.
