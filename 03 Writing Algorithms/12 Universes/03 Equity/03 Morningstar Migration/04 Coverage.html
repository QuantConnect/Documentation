The new dataset starts in January 1998, like the old one, and covers more US Equities. A larger dataset changes universe selection, even for companies whose values didn't change. For example, if you select the 500 companies with the largest dollar volume that have fundamental data, the selection now comes from a larger pool. Companies near the cut-off can drop out of your universe. The HasFundamentalDatahas_fundamental_data property works as before and returns trueTrue for more securities.
