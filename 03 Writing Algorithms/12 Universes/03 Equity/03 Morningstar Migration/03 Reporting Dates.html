The old feeds dated the ratio, growth, per-share, and average properties at the end of the reporting period. The company filed the statement behind these values weeks later. In the new dataset, these properties take the filing date of the statement they come from. Most of their values now arrive later than before, by a median of 63 days.

Backtests and research on the old dataset could read these values before they were public. Live algorithms only receive the data that the vendor delivers each day, so they never received these values early. Strategies that rebalance soon after the end of a fiscal period now wait for the filing before they receive the new values.
