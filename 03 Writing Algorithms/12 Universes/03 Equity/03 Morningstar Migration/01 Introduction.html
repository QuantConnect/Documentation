Morningstar retired the Global Equity Data Feeds that supplied the US Fundamental Data dataset and replaced them with the Equity Data Feeds. QuantConnect rebuilt the entire history of the dataset from the new feeds. Backtests that ran on the old dataset can produce different results on the new dataset.

The old dataset remains on the master branch of LEAN until October 10, 2026. From October 10 through the end of October, the master branch uses the new dataset and the old dataset is available on a separate branch. To use the new dataset now, change the LEAN engine branch of your project. The old dataset will be fully deprecated for backtesting and live trading on October 31, 2026.
