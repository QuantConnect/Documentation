To get historical US Bank Financials data, call the Historyhistory method with the dataset Symbol. If there is no data in the period you request, the history result is empty.

history = self.history[FDICBankFinancials](self._dataset_symbol, timedelta(days=365), Resolution.DAILY)
var history = History<FDICBankFinancials>(_datasetSymbol, TimeSpan.FromDays(365), Resolution.Daily);

For more information about historical data, see History Requests.
