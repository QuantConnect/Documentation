The US Bank Financials dataset by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) tracks the quarterly call report of every FDIC-insured bank, with its balance sheet, deposits, loans, asset quality, and regulatory capital. The data covers 24,064 banks, starts in June 1984, and is delivered on a quarterly frequency. This dataset is created by parsing the call reports the FDIC publishes. The banks of listed companies are linked to 644 US Equities from March 1998.
