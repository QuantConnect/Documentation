The US Bank Failures dataset by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) tracks the failures of FDIC-insured banks, with the assets and deposits of each bank and how the FDIC resolved it. The data covers 4,117 bank failures, starts in April 1934, and is delivered on a daily frequency. This dataset is created by parsing the list of failed banks the FDIC publishes. The data does not include the FDIC's estimated cost of each failure.

For more information about the US Bank Failures dataset, including CLI commands and pricing, see the dataset listing.
