The OTC Transparency dataset by the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) tracks short interest and off-exchange trading volume for US Equities. The data covers 59,712 US Equities, starts in January 2017, and is delivered on a daily frequency. This dataset is created by processing the short interest and off-exchange volume reports that broker-dealers file with FINRA.

For more information about the OTC Transparency dataset, including CLI commands and pricing, see the dataset listing.
