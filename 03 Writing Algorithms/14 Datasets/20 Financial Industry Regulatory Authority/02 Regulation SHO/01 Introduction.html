The Regulation SHO dataset by the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) tracks the daily off-exchange short sale volume of US Equities. The data covers 22,073 US Equities, starts in August 2018, and is delivered on a daily frequency. This dataset is created by processing the short sale volume that broker-dealers report to FINRA under Regulation SHO.

For more information about the Regulation SHO dataset, including CLI commands and pricing, see the dataset listing.
