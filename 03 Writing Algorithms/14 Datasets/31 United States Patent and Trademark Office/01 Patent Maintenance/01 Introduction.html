The Patent Maintenance dataset by the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) tracks the patent renewals and lapses of US-listed companies. The data covers 2,896 US Equities, starts in April 1985, and is delivered on a weekly frequency. This dataset is created by combining the USPTO maintenance fee records with PatentsView assignee data and mapping each patent owner to its ticker.

This dataset depends on the US Equity Security Master dataset because the US Equity Security Master dataset contains information on splits, dividends, and symbol changes.
