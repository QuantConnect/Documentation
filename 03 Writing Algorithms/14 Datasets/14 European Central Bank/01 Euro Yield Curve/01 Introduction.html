The Euro Area Yield Curve dataset by the European Central Bank (ECB) tracks the yield curve of euro area government bonds. The data covers 6 yield curves at 10 maturities from 3 months to 30 years, starts in September 2004, and is delivered on a daily frequency. This dataset is created by the ECB fitting a Svensson model to the prices of euro area government bonds.

For more information about the Euro Yield Curve dataset, including CLI commands and pricing, see the dataset listing.
