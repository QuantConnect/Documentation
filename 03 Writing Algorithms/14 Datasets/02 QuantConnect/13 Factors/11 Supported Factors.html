The Factors and FactorsUniverse objects behave like a dictionary, mapping the factor name to its value. The Factors class provides name constants for every factor.

Linked Factors

Linked factors are factors that are tied to a specific tradable security. The Factors dataset provides the following linked factors for each US Equity:

  • Factors.ROC_1_DAYFactors.Roc1Day: 1-day rate of change of the closing price: close[t] / close[t-1] - 1
  • Factors.VOLATILITY_21_DAYFactors.Volatility21Day: 21-day realized volatility of daily returns: the standard deviation of 1-day closing-price returns over the trailing 21 days
  • Factors.VOLUME_Z_21_DAYFactors.VolumeZ21Day: 21-day volume z-score: the number of standard deviations the current trading volume sits above or below its trailing 21-day mean
  • Factors.MFI_21_DAYFactors.Mfi21Day: 21-day Money Flow Index: volume-weighted buying vs. selling pressure, from daily typical price changes signed and weighted by volume, accumulated over 21 sessions and bounded 0-100
  • Factors.MOM_12_MONTHFactors.Mom12Month: Twelve-month momentum skipping the most recent month: the stock's return over months t-11 through t-1, computed each session from LEAN's adjusted daily closes, from Jegadeesh and Titman (1993, JF), where higher values predicted higher returns
  • Factors.INT_MOMFactors.IntMom: Intermediate momentum: the stock's return over months t-12 through t-7, computed each session from LEAN's adjusted daily closes, from Novy-Marx (2012, JFE), where higher values predicted higher returns
  • Factors.MOM_12_MONTH_OFF_SEASONFactors.Mom12MonthOffSeason: Momentum without the seasonal month: the mean of the monthly returns of months t-1 through t-10, which leaves out the current month and month t-11, the month one year before the coming month, computed each session from LEAN's adjusted daily closes, from Heston and Sadka (2008, JFE), where higher values predicted higher returns
  • Factors.VOLUME_TRENDFactors.VolumeTrend: Volume trend: the slope of a regression of monthly volume on a linear time trend over the trailing 60 months, divided by the mean monthly volume over those months, computed each session from LEAN's split- and dividend-adjusted daily volume with 21 sessions to a month and at least 30 months required, from Haugen and Baker (1996, JFE), where higher values predicted lower returns
  • Factors.MOM_SEASON_SHORTFactors.MomSeasonShort: Return seasonality last year: the stock's return over the 21 sessions from 252 to 231 sessions ago, the month one year before the coming month, computed each session from LEAN's adjusted daily closes, from Heston and Sadka (2008, JFE), where higher values predicted higher returns
  • Factors.ILLIQUIDITYFactors.Illiquidity: Amihud illiquidity: the mean over the past 12 months of the daily ratio of the absolute return to the dollar volume traded, computed each session from LEAN's adjusted daily bars, from Amihud (2002, JFM), where higher values predicted higher returns
  • Factors.FINRA_SHORT_SALE_VOLUME_SHORT_VOLUME_RATIO_SMA_21_DAYFactors.FinraShortSaleVolumeShortVolumeRatioSma21Day: 21-day simple moving average of the FINRA Reg SHO daily short volume ratio: the share (0-1) of a security's off-exchange reported volume executed as short sales during regular trading hours
  • Factors.SPECTRAL_TICK_FLOW_SIGNAL_EXECUTION_SCOREFactors.SpectralTickFlowSignalExecutionScore: Normalized strength (non-negative) of the strongest recurring intraday execution rhythm in the security's daily tick flow, from a Fourier transform-like spectral decomposition of daily trades; higher means trade flow more strongly backed by an active, repeating execution signature
  • Factors.EARNING_YIELDFactors.EarningYield: Trailing earnings yield: Morningstar's ValuationRatios.EarningYield, diluted EPS divided by price (the inverse trailing P/E), so higher values indicate cheaper stocks relative to trailing earnings

Unlinked Factors

Unlinked factors are factors that aren't tied to a specific tradable security, like weather data or interest rates. The Factors dataset provides the following unlinked factors:

  • Factors.UST_YIELD_1_MONTHFactors.UstYield1Month: 1-month US Treasury yield curve rate, as published daily at 4:30 PM New York time
  • Factors.VIXFactors.Vix: CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) daily close: the market's expectation of 30-day S&P 500 volatility implied by index option prices, in annualized percentage points
  • Factors.WTI_CRUDE_OILFactors.WtiCrudeOil: WTI crude oil price: the daily mid close of Oanda's West Texas Oil CFD, in US dollars per barrel, a continuous quote that tracks the front NYMEX contract without roll jumps
  • Factors.MONETARY_CYCLEFactors.MonetaryCycle: Monetary cycle: the monetary environment of Jensen, Mercer and Johnson (1996), +1 while the most recent change in the Federal Reserve's primary credit rate was a decrease (expansive) and -1 while it was an increase (restrictive), read from LEAN's risk-free interest rate model for every calendar day at midnight New York; the state flips on the day the model's rate changes
  • Factors.M_2_GROWTH_1_MONTHFactors.M2Growth1Month: M2 money supply growth from one monthly release to the next: the Federal Reserve's seasonally adjusted monthly M2 over its value for the month before, minus one, from FRED's series M2SL as currently revised; each month's value is stamped at 13:00 New York on the fourth Tuesday of the following month, when the Federal Reserve's H.6 release publishes it
