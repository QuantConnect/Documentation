Dividend Attributes

Dividend events are triggered on the ex-dividend date. The ReferencePricereference_price of the event is the closing price of the previous trading day. The dataset doesn't include the payment date. Special and capital gains distributions arrive as regular Dividend events with no field to distinguish them.
