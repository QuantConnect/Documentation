Morningstar retired the Global Equity Data Feeds behind this dataset and replaced them with the Equity Data Feeds. From September 23, 2026, US fundamentals come from the new feeds for the whole history, not just for new data, so backtest results change.

  • Of the values both generations carry, about one third differ. Most are figures Morningstar recalculated; the rest come from sign conventions and rounding. Where a value differs, it usually differs by more than 20%.
  • Ratios, growth, per-share and average properties are dated by the filing that produced them instead of by the period end, a median of 63 days later. A backtest that reads them no longer sees a figure before it was public, which is what research and live trading already did.
  • Price multiples update daily instead of monthly, so they move more often.
  • Coverage grows from 8,717 to 9,705 companies. 535 properties are new and 1,251 of the old ones are gone, 358 of which were never populated. A retired property still compiles, but throws when you read it and names its replacement where one exists.

The previous data is deprecated and cannot be kept alongside the new data, so backtests run on it will not reproduce. For the full breakdown of what changed, see Morningstar Migration.
