The Commitments of Traders dataset by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) tracks the long and short positions that each type of trader holds in US futures markets. The data covers 942 futures markets, starts in January 1998, and is delivered on a weekly frequency. This dataset is created by processing the weekly Commitments of Traders reports.

For more information about the Commitments of Traders dataset, including CLI commands and pricing, see the dataset listing.
