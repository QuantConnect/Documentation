The AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel applies the delayed settlement rules and converts the settled cash into your account currency. It credits sale proceeds to your account currency after the settlement period instead of leaving a balance in the instrument's quote currency.

security.SetSettlementModel(new AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel(7, TimeSpan.FromHours(8)));
security.set_settlement_model(AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel(7, timedelta(hours=8)))

For more information about this model, see the class reference and implementation.
