The Trading212BrokerageModel uses the AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel with the T+1 default settlement rules of US Equities. Trading 212 converts every trade to your account currency, so cash settles in the account currency instead of the instrument's quote currency.

A buy debits your account currency immediately. Sale proceeds stay unsettled for one business day and then settle in your account currency.

security.SetSettlementModel(new AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel(Equity.DefaultSettlementDays, Equity.DefaultSettlementTime));
security.set_settlement_model(AccountCurrencyDelayedSettlementModel(Equity.DEFAULT_SETTLEMENT_DAYS, Equity.DEFAULT_SETTLEMENT_TIME))
