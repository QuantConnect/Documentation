This page explains the Trading212BrokerageModel, including the asset classes it supports, its default security-level models, and its default markets.

SetBrokerageModel(BrokerageName.Trading212, AccountType.Cash);
self.set_brokerage_model(BrokerageName.TRADING_212, AccountType.CASH)

The model applies the rules of the Trading 212 Invest, Stocks ISA, and SIPP accounts. Trading 212 doesn't have a live brokerage integration, so you can use the model for backtesting and paper trading.
