The EtoroBrokerageModel uses the CashBuyingPowerModel for Crypto and Forex trades in cash accounts and the SecurityMarginModel for all other cases.

If you have a cash account, the EtoroBrokerageModel sets the leverage of every security to 1. If you have a margin account, it sets the default leverage to 2x for Equities, 1x for Crypto, and 30x for Forex. To open a position with leverage above 1, attach a stop loss to the entry order.
