The EtoroFeeModel models the fees of eToro.

security.SetFeeModel(new EtoroFeeModel());
security.set_fee_model(EtoroFeeModel())

The EtoroFeeModel charges $0 USD for Equity and Forex trades. For Crypto trades, it charges 1% of the trade value on each buy and sell, in the quote currency of the pair.
