The Trading212FeeModel models the fees of Trading 212. Trading 212 is commission free, so the model charges an FX fee and the US regulatory fees on Equity sales.

security.SetFeeModel(new Trading212FeeModel());
security.set_fee_model(Trading212FeeModel())

The following table describes the fees:

Fee Amount Applies to
FX 0.15% of the converted notional Buys and sells of instruments that are priced in a currency other than your account currency
SEC Section 31 0.206 basis points ($20.60 per $1,000,000) of the sale value Sales of US-listed Equities
FINRA trading activity $0.000195 per share, capped at $9.79 per trade Sales of US-listed Equities

The SEC Section 31 rate applies from April 4, 2026 and the FINRA trading activity fee applies from January 1, 2026. Both agencies reset the rates on a published schedule.

The model settles the fees in your account currency, so a fee never leaves a balance in the instrument's quote currency.

The model assumes a GBP account currency by default, which matches a UK Stocks and Shares ISA. If your account uses a different currency, pass that currency to the constructor:

security.SetFeeModel(new Trading212FeeModel(Currencies.USD));
security.set_fee_model(Trading212FeeModel(Currencies.USD))
