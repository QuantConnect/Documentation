To get the historical data of the CrossSectionalInformationCoefficient indicator, call the IndicatorHistoryself.indicator_history method. This method resets your indicator, makes a history request, and updates the indicator with the historical data. Just like with regular history requests, the IndicatorHistoryindicator_history method supports time periods based on a trailing number of bars, a trailing period of time, or a defined period of time. If you don't provide a resolution argument, it defaults to match the resolution of the security subscription.

        var indicatorHistory = IndicatorHistory(_csic, _symbols, 100, Resolution.Minute);

        var timePeriodIndicatorHistory = IndicatorHistory(_csic, _symbols, new DateTime(2024, 7, 1), new DateTime(2024, 7, 5), Resolution.Minute);
    }
}
