The QuantConnect data provider sources US Index Options market data from the Options Price Reporting Authority (OPRA), which consolidates the last sale and quotation information that originates from the national securities exchanges that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approves.

Unlike Equity Options, Index Options are proprietary products, so the contracts on each index only trade on the exchange group that licenses the index. Cboe lists the SPX and VIX contracts, and Nasdaq lists the NDX contracts on its PHLX, ISE, and GEMX exchanges.
