You can update your subscription plan to add or remove products, such as seats, nodes, add-ons, data, and support, to switch between monthly and annual billing, or to change your billing date. You need billing permissions in the organization to update the plan.

Follow these steps to update your subscription plan:

  1. In the top navigation bar, click Pricing.
  2. On the Customize Plan tab, add or remove products.
  3. Select monthly or annual billing.
  4. Review the Billing Summary. It shows the price and discount of every product and the total you'll pay. To compare it with what you pay now, see your previous invoice on the Organization > Billing page.
  5. Click Proceed to Checkout.

Renewal and Proration

Every plan update renews your entire subscription for a new billing period. On monthly billing, the unused part of your current period is credited, and we charge the price of the new subscription minus that credit. If the credit is larger than the new price, the rest stays on your account and is applied to future invoices.

For example, the Researcher pack (a Researcher seat, B2-8 backtesting node, R1-4 research node, L-MICRO live trading node, and A1-1 agent node) costs $84 per month and each B2-8 node costs $14 per month. If your billing date is the 28th of a 30-day month and you update your plan on the 10th, 12 days are used and 18 days are unused:

  • If you add a second B2-8 node, the unused credit is $84 × 18 / 30 = $50.40, the new subscription costs $84 + $14 = $98, and you pay $98 - $50.40 = $47.60.
  • If you have two B2-8 nodes and remove one, the unused credit is $98 × 18 / 30 = $58.80, the new subscription costs $84, and you pay $84 - $58.80 = $25.20.
  • If you only need live trading and remove the B2-8, R1-4, and A1-1 nodes, the unused credit is $84 × 18 / 30 = $50.40 and the new subscription costs $10 + $24 = $34 for the seat and the L-MICRO node. You pay $0 and the remaining $50.40 - $34 = $16.40 stays on your account, so on the next 10th you pay $34 - $16.40 = $17.60.

In each case, your next billing date is the 10th.

Billing Date

Since every plan update starts a new billing period, the day you check out becomes your new billing date. To change your billing date, check out on the day you want without changing any products.

For example, if your $84 per month Researcher pack renews on the 28th of a 30-day month and you want to be billed on the 10th, check out on the 10th. The 18 unused days are credited, $84 × 18 / 30 = $50.40, so you pay $84 - $50.40 = $33.60. From then on, you pay $84 on the 10th of every month.

Price Changes

If we change the price of a product in your subscription, you keep your current price for some time. When you update your plan in any way, including when you only switch the billing frequency or change the billing date, every product renews at the current prices.

Annual Billing

The billing frequency applies to your entire subscription, so you can't bill some products monthly and others annually. If you update your plan or cancel your subscription before the 12-month term ends, we convert the months you used to monthly billing at the monthly price, without discounts, and the rest of your annual payment becomes credit on your account, which is applied to future invoices. This credit isn't QuantConnect Credit. If you keep annual billing, your subscription renews for a new 12-month term with the annual discount but without the coupon discount.

For example, the Researcher pack (a Researcher seat, B2-8 backtesting node, R1-4 research node, L-MICRO live trading node, and A1-1 agent node) costs $84 per month, $844 per year on annual billing, and $756 per year with a coupon. If you pay $756 and update your plan after 6 months, the 6 months you used are charged at $84 each ($504), which leaves $252 of credit. If you cancel or switch to monthly billing, the $252 is applied to your future invoices. If you keep annual billing and add a second B2-8 node, your subscription renews for a new 12-month term at $844 + $144 = $988 and you pay $988 - $252 = $736.
