You need billing permissions in the organization to add nodes.

Adding a node renews your entire subscription period. We charge you the difference in price between your current subscription and the new one, which covers both the node and the extension of the subscription.

If your organization has an annual plan and you add a node before the 12-month term ends, we convert the months you used to monthly billing at the monthly price, without discounts, and credit the rest of your annual payment against a new 12-month term at the current prices, without the coupon discount. For more information, see Update Subscription Plan.

Follow these steps to add nodes to your organization:

  1. Open the Resources page.
  2. Click Add nodeType Node for the type of node you want to add.
  3. Select the node specifications.
  4. Click Add Node.

    5. The Resources page displays the new node.
