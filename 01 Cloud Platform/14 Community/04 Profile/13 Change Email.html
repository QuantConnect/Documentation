Follow these steps to change your email:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. In the top navigation bar, click yourUsername > My Account.
  3. On your Account page, in the Security section, click Change Email.
  4. Enter your current email.
  5. Enter your new email.
  6. Enter your new email again to confirm.
  7. Click Change Email.
  8. Check the inbox of your current email address and click Change Email to confirm.

The confirmation email goes to your current email address, so you need access to it to complete the change. For security, support only changes the email of an account when the request comes from the email address registered to the account. If you can't access your current email address, create a new account with an email address you control.
