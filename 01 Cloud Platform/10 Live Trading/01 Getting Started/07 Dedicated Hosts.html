A dedicated host is a server that we provision for your organization alone. It provides the equivalent of 80 usable cores and 160 GB of RAM, which we currently configure as 20 L4-8 live trading nodes. Because the host isn't shared with other members, the nodes on it always have their full resources available.

Dedicated hosts are available in the New York (NY7) and Toronto (TOR) regions. To request one, contact us.
