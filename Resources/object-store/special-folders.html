Your organization's Object Store includes a reserved .agent folder that the QuantConnect AI agents read. It holds the custom skills, memories, and templates you define for your organization, which let the agents apply your own expertise, remember your preferences, and start new projects from your own scaffolds.

The .agent folder contains the following subfolders:

SubfolderPurpose
skills Reusable instructions and conventions the agents apply.
memories Facts the agents remember across conversations.
templates Your own project scaffolds for the agents to initialize for new projects.
