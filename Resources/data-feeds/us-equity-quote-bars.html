Within a day, each quote bar starts from the closing bid, closing ask, and last sizes of the previous bar. Then it aggregates the quotes of its own period. If a period has quotes on only one side, the other side keeps the closing price and last size of the previous bar. The first bar of each day doesn't take values from the previous day, so each side stays empty until its first quote of the day.

The closing bid of a quote bar is never above its closing ask. If a period ends with the closing bid above the closing ask, one side takes the closing price and last size of the other side. In this case, the bid and ask have the same closing price and size.
