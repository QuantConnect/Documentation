Each quote bar starts from the closing bid, closing ask, and last sizes of the previous bar. Then it aggregates the quotes of its own period. If a period has quotes on only one side, the other side keeps the closing price and last size of the previous bar.

The closing bid of a quote bar is never above its closing ask. If the quotes of a period leave the closing bid above the closing ask, the bar adjusts the side that the last quote didn't update. That side takes the closing price and last size of the other side, so the bid and ask have the same closing price and size.
