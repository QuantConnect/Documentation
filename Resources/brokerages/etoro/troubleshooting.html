The following table describes errors you may see when deploying to eToro:

Error Message(s) Possible Cause and Fix
No acknowledgement of the authentication or of the subscription to the order updates arrived within 30 seconds.
 Your API key or user key may be incorrect, expired, or created for the other environment. A user key works for either the Demo or the Real environment. Check the key in Settings > Trading > API Key Management on eToro and select the matching environment when you deploy.
A short sale of <ticker> at eToro needs a stop loss
 eToro requires a stop loss on every short position. Open the short position with a bracket order or a one triggers other order that triggers a stop market order.
A position in <ticker> at leverage <leverage> at eToro needs a stop loss
 eToro requires a stop loss on every position with leverage above 1. Attach a stop market order to the entry order, or set the leverage of the security to 1.
The stop loss of <ticker> at eToro has to be at least 10% away from the price
 eToro keeps the stop loss of a Crypto position at least 10% away from the current price. Move the stop price further from the current price.
The stop loss of <ticker> at <price> is on the wrong side of the price
The take profit of <ticker> at <price> is on the wrong side of the price
 For a long position, set the stop loss below the current price and the take profit above it. For a short position, set the stop loss above the current price and the take profit below it.
No instrument '<ticker>' of '<symbol>' is listed on the supported exchanges.
 eToro doesn't list the security. Check the ticker in the eToro app and trade a security that eToro lists.

If you need further support, open a new support ticket and add the live deployment with the error.
