LEAN subscribes to every contract that passes your filter, so you don't need to call the AddFutureContractadd_future_contract method for them. To trade a specific contract, such as when you manage the rollover with your own rule, select it from the FuturesChain in the Slice and place your orders with its Symbol. Set the filter wide enough to include the next contract on the date you plan to roll. For example, a week before the front-month contract expires, the next quarterly contract is about 98 days away, so a filter of 0 to 92 days excludes it.
