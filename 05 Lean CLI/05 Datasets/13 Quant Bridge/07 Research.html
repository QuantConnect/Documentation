To access Bloomberg DAPI data from the local Research Environment through Quant Bridge, open a terminal in your organization workspace and then run lean research <projectName> --data-provider-historical "Quant Bridge" --quant-bridge-connection-type DAPI --quant-bridge-environment <environment> --quant-bridge-server-host <serverHost> --quant-bridge-server-port <serverPort> --quant-bridge-emsx-broker <emsxBroker> --quant-bridge-openfigi-api-key <openfigiApiKey>.

$ lean research "My Project" --data-provider-historical "Quant Bridge" --quant-bridge-connection-type DAPI --quant-bridge-environment Production --quant-bridge-server-host serverHost --quant-bridge-server-port serverPort --quant-bridge-emsx-broker emsxBroker --quant-bridge-openfigi-api-key openfigiApiKey

If you provide any of the preceding options, your Lean configuration file saves them so that you only need to run lean research <projectName> to open the Research Environment with the same options.
